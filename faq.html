What is a Real Estate Negotiator (REN) in Malaysia?

A Real Estate Negotiator (REN) is an individual registered with Malaysia's Board of Valuers, Appraisers, Estate Agents and Property Managers (BOVAEP) to market and negotiate property deals under a licensed real estate agency. Always ask an agent for their REN number before engaging their services.
