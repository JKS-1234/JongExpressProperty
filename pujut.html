Why Invest or Live in Pujut?

Pujut is a premier mature suburb in Miri, Sarawak, stretching across Pujut 1 through Pujut 7. Residents benefit from unbeatable connectivity between Miri City Centre, Lutong, and Senadin. Located minutes away from GK Supermarket, Boulevard Commercial Centre, Saberkas Night Market, and top local primary and secondary schools, Pujut remains one of the highest-demand locations for families, professionals, and long-term property investors.
