Why Invest or Live in Senadin?

Senadin is a rapidly growing township renowned as Miri's premier educational and industrial corridor. Home to Curtin University Malaysia and Knewton Global Schools, it offers exceptional rental yield opportunities for student and faculty housing. With quick access to the Kuala Baram industrial estate, Nam Leong commercial areas, and affordable residential phases, Senadin is ideal for both first-time homebuyers and savvy investors.
