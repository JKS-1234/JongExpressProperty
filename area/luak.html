About Luak

Luak sits on Miri's coastal side, close to the airport, and is known for newer developer-launched housing such as tropical-style single and double storey semi-detached homes. It appeals to buyers who want a modern house at an accessible price while staying within easy reach of schools, shopping and entertainment.
