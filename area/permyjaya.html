About Permyjaya

Permyjaya is a fast-growing suburb anchored by Permy Mall and KPJ Miri Specialist Hospital, and it includes the Desa Pujut, Desa Bahagia, Desa Murni and Southlake neighbourhoods. Properties here range from single-storey detached houses on generous land to bungalows and inter shoplots with ample parking, all within a short drive of Boulevard and the city.
