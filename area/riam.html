About Riam

Riam is a convenient residential belt close to Miri Airport, Emart Riam shopping mall and Miri General Hospital, covering Taman Tunku, Prosperity Garden, Serene Heights and nearby neighbourhoods. Terrace and semi-detached houses, condos and commercial lots here suit families, airline and hospital staff, and investors who want easy access to the town centre.
