About Krokop

Krokop is an established neighbourhood between Pujut and Piasau, known for its local food spots, Pei Min Middle School and quick access to Boulevard Shopping Mall. Double-storey semi-detached houses, including the Jee Foh area, are the typical offering and are popular with families and tenants.

We currently list 3 properties in Krokop (3 houses). Asking prices for sale run from RM 950,000 to RM 1,100,000 and monthly rents run from RM 4,000.
