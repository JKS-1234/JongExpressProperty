About Lutong

Lutong, north of Miri city, has long been home to oil and gas staff working at the nearby Shell and Petronas offices. It is quieter and more spacious than the town centre, with detached and semi-detached houses, inter shoplots near local supermarkets and malls, and neighbourhoods such as Taman Bayshore. Expect roughly 20 minutes to Miri town.

We currently list 3 properties in Lutong (2 houses, 1 shoplot). Asking prices for sale run from RM 728,000 to RM 1,000,000.
